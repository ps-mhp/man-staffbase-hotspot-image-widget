# Step by step

## Set up the basic case

1. Place the **Image with Dots** widget on the page. 
2. Open the widget's settings. The window for editing the
   Dots opens by itself. 
3. Click on **Choose Image...** on the left and select an image from the
   Media Library. You can't set a point without a picture. 
4. Enter an **alternative text** underneath that describes what is written on
   can be seen in the picture. 
5. Click on the desired location in the image. A new
   Period, and his form appears on the right. 
6. Enter the **title**. Without a title, the window cannot be used with
   **Apply** close — a corresponding notice will appear both on
   field and at the bottom of the window. 
7. Optionally, enter a **description**. 
8. Optional: add under the "Description" field via **link
   add** a target — see the section "Setting a link" continue
   below. 
9. Repeat steps 5 through 8 for each additional point. A picture
   carries a maximum of 20 points. 
10. Click on **Apply**. 
11. Under **Appearance**, choose between "Numbered, with list next to
    the picture" and "Points, without list". 
12. Save the page and preview the result. 

## Set a link

1. Click on **Add link** in the form of a point. 
2. Under **Destination**, select a page from the list. A link to a page
   from this list will appear later in a window above the page. 
3. If you want to enter your own address instead, click on
   **Enter your own address ...** and enter the address in the field
   **Address**. Such an address will open later in a new
   Tab, not in the window — that's what the information text is for. 
4. By **Select a page instead** you will come from your own
   Address back to list. 
5. Optionally, enter your own **label of the button**. Remains
   the field is empty, the button shows "Open page". 
6. Via **Remove link** the link disappears again; Title and
   description of the point remain unaffected by this. 

## Move, sort and remove points

1. To move a point, drag it to the new location in the image. 
2. To change the order of the numbering, select the period in the
   Select the list on the right and click on **Up** or **Move Down**. 
3. To remove a point, select it and click in its
   Delete form to **Period**. 
4. Complete each change with **Apply**; via **Cancel** 
   discard all changes since the last time the window was opened. 

## Change afterwards

1. Open the widget's settings again. The window will show the
   existing points and the selected image. 
2. Select the item you want to change in the list on the left and edit
   You can use the form on the right. 
3. To replace the image, click **Choose another image...**. 
   The points are retained; then check whether their position on the
   new picture still fits. 
4. **Apply**, save, preview. 

## When something doesn't work

1. **The widget doesn't show anything.** Either the image is missing, or it's still
   no dot set. Open Settings and check both. 
2. **"Apply" cannot be clicked.** At least one point still has
   no title. The window at the bottom states how many points are affected — 
   enter one title at a time. 
3. **The linked page does not appear in the window, but opens a
   new tab.** The goal is a self-entered address, not a page from
   list. This is how it is intended: foreign pages often cannot be
   embed a window. Instead, select a page from the list 
   if you want it to appear in the window. 
4. **In the list of pages there is not a single page, but a field for
   an address.** The system's page list was not open when it was opened.
   reachable. Enter the address by hand or open the
   window again.
